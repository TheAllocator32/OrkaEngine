### What is this folder?

This folder contains Orka’s primitives.

### What are primitives?

In most contexts, primitives are simple types such as `int` or `float`.

In Orka, some types, such as `vector3df`, `Texture`, `Sound` and `GUIPage`, also behave like value types. For that reason, they are treated as primitives here.

### Why not call them value types?

Because “primitive” sounds more dramatic.

### Why are textures primitives?

Because they behave like values in this codebase.
